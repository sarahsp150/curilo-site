Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2025

curilo is a browser extension designed with a “privacy-first” approach. We believe your shopping habits should remain your own.

What curilo does

curilo lets you organize your favorite online shops on a new tab page and save product images from any website to a personal wishlist.

Data storage

All data is stored locally on your device using the Chrome storage API. Your data never leaves your browser.

Data collection

curilo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no servers.

Web page interaction

When you hover over a product image, curilo reads the image URL and basic product info from the page. This data is only stored locally if you save the item.

External requests

curilo makes the following requests (no personal data transmitted):

  • Shop logos: From logo.clearbit.com and Google Favicons (only domain name sent).
  • Fonts: Jost and Cormorant Garamond fonts from Google Fonts.

Affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate links. If you purchase through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Permissions

  • storage: Save shops, wishlist items, settings locally.
  • activeTab: Open image picker on current page.
  • Host permission (all URLs): Display save button on all shopping websites.

Third-party services

curilo does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Children’s privacy

curilo does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be reflected here with an updated date.

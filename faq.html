FAQ

Everything you need to know about curating with curilo.

General

What is curilo? +
curilo is a free browser extension that replaces your new tab page with a personalised shopping start page. Organise your favourite online shops and save product images from any website to your wishlist with a single click.
Is curilo free? +
Yes, completely free.
Does curilo work on every website? +
curilo works on most online shops. The save button appears automatically on product images. If it doesn’t show on a specific page, click the curilo icon in the toolbar to open the image picker.

Wishlist

How do I save an item? +
Hover over a product image in any online shop and click the ‘Save’ button — the item is instantly added to your wishlist.
Can I create multiple wishlists? +
Yes. In the settings you can add more wishlists and name them directly.
Can I change the display size? +
Yes. Default is M. In settings switch between S and L.
How do I delete an item? +
Hover over the item — a small ‘x’ appears in the top right corner. Click it.
Can I collapse or hide wishlists? +
Collapse: Click the triangle next to the section. Hide: Go to settings under ‘Sections’.

Shops & Dashboard

How do I add shops? +
Click the ‘+’ icon at the end of the shop list and enter the URL. Name and icon are generated automatically.
How do I organise my shops? +
Create categories and sort shops via drag & drop. Click a shop logo to visit it directly.
Can I sort or reorder shops? +
Yes. In settings you can reorder shops and categories via drag & drop.
Can I collapse or hide shops? +
Collapse: Click the triangle. Hide: Settings under ‘Sections’.

Save Button

How does the Save button work? +
Hover over a product image on any shop — a ‘Save’ button appears in the corner. Click it to save the product.
Why don’t I see the button? +
Some domains are excluded. On some shops detection may vary depending on page structure.
Can I change the button position? +
Yes. Settings let you choose: top left, top right, bottom left, or bottom right.

Settings & Privacy

Can I back up my data? +
Yes. Export/import in settings lets you save data as JSON and restore on any device.
Where is my data stored? +
All data is stored locally on your device. It never leaves your browser.
Does curilo collect personal data? +
No. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no servers.
